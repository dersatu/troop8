About the Troop

Troop 8 meets weekly at Ogden Memorial Presbyterian Church in Chatham, New Jersey. We offer camping, hiking, backpacking, service projects, high-adventure trips, merit badges, and leadership opportunities for Scouts of all experience levels.

Interested in Joining?

Families interested in learning more are always welcome. Visit our main troop website using the button above or contact us by email to attend a meeting.
